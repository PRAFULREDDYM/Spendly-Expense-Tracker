Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker syncs your workspace through Supabase so the same account is available on your phone, tablet, and laptop. Signed-in devices also keep an offline cache for fast access when connectivity is limited.

What we collect

  • Account, finance, and receipt data you enter is stored in your Supabase-backed workspace.
  • Signed-in devices cache recent workspace data locally to support offline viewing.
  • Hosting providers may still record standard web logs for availability and security.

How we use data

  • To render your finance workspace, charts, history, and quick-add shortcuts across devices.
  • To sync your categories, budgets, expenses, preferences, and receipts across signed-in devices.
  • To support backup and restore files only when you explicitly export or import them.
  • To enable installable PWA behavior and quick-add entry points.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use your finance data for advertising, profiling, or third-party analytics.

Your data syncs through your own authenticated account and is cached locally for offline use. We do not sell or share that financial data with third parties.
